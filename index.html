01 / THE HOMECOMING

Sama-sama ulit

A school fair, reimagined for the family we became.

Hosted by ACD Batch 2001 together with the Golden Jubilarians of Batch 1976, Sama-Sama Tayo brings hundreds of alumni together for an evening of reconnection, gratitude, and celebration.

Relive the spirit of ACD’s beloved annual Sama-Sama—with music and entertainment, activity booths, food stalls, raffle prizes, and stories that pick up right where they left off.

02 / A HOMECOMING WITH HEART

When we gather, we give back.

Proceeds from the homecoming help support communities and learners through two meaningful initiatives.

✦Community care

Purok Lawis

Chapel renovation and a medical mission for the fisherfolk community of Brgy. Lizada, Daliao, Toril.

●Education access

Lumad learners

Support for free education, classroom repairs, facilities, and school supplies in Tamugan and Panalum.
